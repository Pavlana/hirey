# Company interview (final round) — question set

Default length: 5 questions, mostly behavioural. Answers use situation, task, action, result.
Score areas (use these names exactly):

- `stories`: concrete examples with the candidate's own actions and a measurable result
- `ownership`: owning outcomes, decisions and mistakes
- `collaboration`: working with others, handling disagreement and feedback
- `values fit`: links answers to what the company says it values
- `questions asked`: thoughtful questions about the team, the work and how success is measured

Questions (pick and adapt; one per reply):

1. Tell me about a project you are proud of. What was your part?
2. Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate or a manager. What happened?
3. Tell me about a mistake you made at work and what you changed afterwards.
4. Describe a time you had to deliver with unclear requirements or a tight deadline.
5. Tell me about a time you helped someone else grow or unblocked a team.
6. Which of our values matters most to you, and when have you shown it?
7. What would you want to have achieved after six months in this role?
8. What questions do you have for us?

Rubric: 5 specific, structured, clear result; 4 good with a missing result or detail; 3 relevant but
generic or "we" not "I"; 2 vague or off the question; 1 no real example.
