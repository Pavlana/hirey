# Recruiter call — question set

Default length: 5 questions. Score areas (use these names exactly):

- `pitch`: a clear two-minute story of background and what comes next
- `motivation`: why this company and this role, with specifics from the posting
- `logistics`: availability, location and working pattern, notice period, right to work; answered plainly
- `questions asked`: good questions back to the recruiter

Questions (pick and adapt to the role; one per reply):

1. Tell me about yourself and what you are looking for next.
2. What do you know about us, and why this role?
3. Walk me through your most recent role: team, scope, what you owned.
4. Why are you leaving, or why did your last role end?
5. What is your notice period, and when could you start?
6. Where are you based, and how do you feel about the office pattern in the posting?
7. Which other processes are you in, and how far along?
8. Do you have any questions for me?

Never ask about current or expected pay in a mock; if the user wants to practise that answer, they say so.

Rubric: 5 crisp and specific, ready as is; 4 good, small polish; 3 adequate but generic or long;
2 vague, rambling or missing key facts; 1 no real answer.
